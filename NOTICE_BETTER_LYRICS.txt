Modified for Nami: Motionartwork model conversion, bounded expiring cache of
hits and misses, coroutine cancellation, HTTPS URL checks, stricter full-artist
identity checking, existing Motion Artwork settings and rendering integration.
No new binary dependency was added. This is part of the GPL-3.0 FOSS build.

Lookup endpoint: https://artwork.boidu.dev/
Only song title (s), artist name (a), and two-letter region (storefront) are
sent. No YouTube ID, login token, cookie, lyrics, library or history is sent.
Artwork URLs returned by the service are played as muted artwork loops.
The external service and returned artwork are not bundled or owned by Nami.
The upstream code license does not guarantee availability or grant ownership
of artwork returned by the external service.
